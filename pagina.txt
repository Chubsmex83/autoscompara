Build a complete, production-ready single-page web application called "AutoCompara MX" — a vehicle comparison tool exclusively for cars and pickup trucks officially sold in Mexico. The app must be written in a single HTML file with embedded CSS and JavaScript (no external frameworks, no build tools, no backend required).

---

## IMPORTANT SCOPE CONSTRAINT

This tool is EXCLUSIVELY for vehicles that are officially sold through authorized dealerships in Mexico as of 2024-2025. All prices must be in Mexican Pesos (MXN) reflecting official MSRP from the Mexican market. Do NOT include vehicles that are not officially available in Mexico (e.g. no US-only trims, no models not distributed in Mexico). All content, labels, units, and UI text must be in Spanish.

---

## UI & LAYOUT

### Header
- App name "AutoCompara MX" with a clean logo icon and a small Mexican flag emoji (🇲🇽) or subtle accent
- Subtitle: "Compara vehículos disponibles en México y toma la mejor decisión de compra"
- Small disclaimer text: "Precios de lista oficiales en México · Modelos disponibles en agencias nacionales"

### Vehicle Selector (always visible at top)
- 3 side-by-side selection panels (slots)
- Each slot has:
  - A searchable select/dropdown to pick a vehicle from the dataset
  - Once selected: show a vehicle card with image placeholder (colored div with car icon), brand/model, price in MXN formatted as currency (e.g. $459,900), and category badge
  - A "✕ Quitar" button to clear the slot
  - An empty state with dashed border and "+ Agregar vehículo" text
- A "Comparar ahora" CTA button once at least 2 vehicles are selected

### Comparison Table (shown after clicking "Comparar")
Full responsive comparison table grouped into sections:

**Precio y Categoría**
- Precio de lista en México (MXN)
- Categoría
- Año / Versión

**Motor y Rendimiento**
- Motor (cilindrada + tipo)
- Potencia (HP)
- Torque (Nm)
- Transmisión
- Tracción
- Consumo ciudad (km/l)
- Consumo carretera (km/l)

**Capacidad y Practicidad**
- Capacidad de cajuela/carga (litros)
- Capacidad de arrastre (kg)
- Número de pasajeros

**Seguridad y Garantía**
- Calificación de seguridad LATINNCAP (estrellas)
- Garantía vigente en México (años)

**Equipamiento Destacado**
- Features como tags/pills

**Ventajas y Desventajas**
- Pros con íconos de palomita verde
- Contras con íconos de X roja

**Red de Distribuidores**
- Cobertura de agencias en México

Table behavior:
- First column sticky (row label)
- Highlight best numeric value per row in green, worst in red/orange
- Works with 2 or 3 vehicles selected

### Sección de Recomendación (below table)
Programmatic JS scoring algorithm that weights:
- Relación precio-valor (precio vs equipamiento)
- Calificación de seguridad
- Rendimiento de combustible
- Practicidad (cajuela + pasajeros)
- Desempeño (HP + torque)

Output:
- Winner card with trophy icon and explanation
- CSS-only horizontal score bars per vehicle per criterion
- 3-4 sentence recommendation paragraph in Spanish
- "¿Para quién es ideal?" use-case tags (e.g. "Familia numerosa", "Ciudad", "Carretera larga", "Terreno difícil", "Primer auto", "Presupuesto ajustado")
